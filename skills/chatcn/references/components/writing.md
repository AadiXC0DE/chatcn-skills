# Writing

Writing tools: suggested changes, tone controls, summary length, meeting summary, doc chat split, deck generator. Install a free item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn/<name>` and a Pro item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn-pro/<name>`. Full docs for any item: `https://chatcn.dev/docs/components/<name>.md`.

## chatcn Pro

- `deck-generator` (Pro): **Deck generator.** Outline to slides, drafted one by one, with themes, per slide layouts, regenerate and export.
- `enhanced-notes` (Pro): **Enhanced notes.** Meeting notes after the assistant reads the transcript: your lines stay in ink, what it added sits a gray step back, editing an added line makes it yours, and every added line jumps to the moment it came from.
- `suggest-changes` (Pro): **Suggest changes.** Track changes from the assistant, set inside the document: insertions underlined in green, deletions struck in red, accept or reject each one, a review strip with one tick per change, and accept all.
- `meeting-summary` (Pro): **Meeting summary.** What a meeting settled: decisions, action items with owners and dates, and open questions, each linked to its moment, with a meeting strip that marks where every item was said.
- `doc-chat-split` (Pro): **Doc chat split.** The document on the left, the chat on the right. A selection in the document becomes a quote chip in the composer, quotes point back to their passage, and answers insert into the document after the passage they are about.
- `tone-controls` (Pro): **Tone controls.** Tone and length for a rewrite on one pad, with a live preview of the rewritten passage, the word count change, the original one press away and Replace passage.
- `side-by-side-translate` (Pro): **Side by side translate.** Source and translation set side by side as prose, paragraph against paragraph, with hover and focus sync: point at a sentence and its counterpart lights up while the rest steps back.
- `summary-length` (Pro): **Summary length.** One summary at several lengths on a stepped ruler, TL;DR to detailed, with word counts under each stop and an instant swap of the text.
