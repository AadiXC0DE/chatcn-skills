# Collaboration

Multiplayer: presence stack, typing presence, live cursors, thread comments, approval routing, handoff to a teammate, agent in a channel. Install a free item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn/<name>` and a Pro item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn-pro/<name>`. Full docs for any item: `https://chatcn.dev/docs/components/<name>.md`.

## chatcn Pro

- `presence-stack` (Pro): **Presence stack.** Who is in a thread, people and agents together, with live state drawn as shapes and a list of what everyone is doing.
- `typing-presence` (Pro): **Typing presence.** Who is typing, as a line of text in the composer footer, with agents described by what they are doing.
- `thread-comments` (Pro): **Thread comments.** Comment on any message, people and agents alike, with resolve that folds the thread to one line.
- `approval-routing` (Pro): **Approval routing.** Who must approve an agent's action, in order, with each step's status and your decision right on your step.
- `handoff-teammate` (Pro): **Handoff to teammate.** Hand a thread to a teammate with a summary they can act on, with the baton showing where the handoff is.
- `agent-in-channel` (Pro): **Agent in channel.** A team channel where several agents reply and hand off to each other, with the handoff drawn in the avatar gutter.
- `team-activity` (Pro): **Team activity.** An activity feed of prompts, runs and approvals across a team that never jumps while you read it.
- `shared-prompts` (Pro): **Shared prompts.** The team's prompt library with owners, use counts and variables that fill into the prompt as you type.
- `follow-mode` (Pro): **Follow mode.** Follow what a teammate or an agent is viewing; scroll yourself and you take over.
- `live-cursors` (Pro): **Live cursors.** Teammates' and agents' cursors over a shared canvas or document, with selections, messages and edge pins.
