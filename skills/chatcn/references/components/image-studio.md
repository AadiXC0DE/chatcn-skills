# Image studio

Image generation apps: imagine bar, prompt builder, generation feed and lightbox, inpaint, outpaint, masks, node canvas, moodboard, upscale compare. Install a free item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn/<name>` and a Pro item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn-pro/<name>`. Full docs for any item: `https://chatcn.dev/docs/components/<name>.md`.

## chatcn Pro

- `palette-extract` (Pro): **Palette extract.** Pull a palette from an image, see where each color lives, preview a palette shift swatch by swatch, lock the colors that must stay and request the shift.
- `character-sheet` (Pro): **Character sheet.** A consistent character from every angle on a turnaround with eye and chin guides, a row of expressions, and views pinned as references for the next generations.
- `layer-stack` (Pro): **Layer stack.** A generated image as layers (subject, background, shadow) with visibility, reorder by drag or keyboard, and regenerating one layer while the rest stay as they are.
- `remix-lineage` (Pro): **Remix lineage.** The family tree of an image: the original, every variation, remix and upscale made from it, and what was made from those. Open any image to ink its line back to the original.
- `describe-image` (Pro): **Describe image.** Reverse prompting: drop, paste or choose an image and get four candidate prompts that describe it. Pick one with 1 to 4, use it as is, edit it in place or copy it.
- `preference-pair` (Pro): **Preference pair.** Two images side by side to train a personal style. 1 or 2 picks the better one, T marks a tie, S skips and Z takes it back, with progress toward a style profile.
- `collection-board` (Pro): **Collection board.** Folders and boards for generated images: drag one image or a whole selection onto a board, multi select with the keys people already know, and act on the selection from a bulk bar with Undo.
- `prompt-history` (Pro): **Prompt history.** Every prompt you ran, grouped by day, with search, a starred filter and a word diff against the prompt you have now, so you see what reusing one would change before you do.
- `node-canvas` (Pro): **Node canvas.** A node graph editor for media workflows: typed ports that only take matching wires, pan, pinch and zoom at the pointer, a minimap, snap to grid, live run state and a complete keyboard path.
- `node-card-set` (Pro): **Node card set.** Seven typed node cards for the node canvas (prompt, image, generate, upscale, mask, rewrite and output), each with live controls and its run state, plus the palette they are added from.
- `image-export-dialog` (Pro): **Image export dialog.** Export generated images: format and quality, size with real pixel dimensions, metadata strip, content credentials with the consequence spelled out, one ZIP or separate files, and an honest size estimate that turns into progress and a result.
- `mask-overlay` (Pro): **Mask overlay.** One image and its mask shown the five ways editors show a selection: marching ants, a tint, the subject isolated against gray, a solid outline, or the black and white matte a model reads. Takes PNG masks, canvases, ImageData or vector paths.
- `click-segment` (Pro): **Click segment.** Click an object to select it, the way promptable segmenters work: positive points pull an object in, negative points push parts out, the mask follows each click, and a crosshair adds points from the keyboard.
- `background-remover` (Pro): **Background remover.** The cutout a matting model made, on a transparency checkerboard, with Keep and Remove brushes to fix its edges, edge softness, background swatches, hold to compare, and a PNG export at the image's natural size.
- `outpaint-canvas` (Pro): **Outpaint canvas.** Zoom out from an image and drag eight handles to extend its canvas. The area the model will fill shows as a dashed frame with its size before anything runs, with aspect presets, Zoom out 1.5× and 2×, extend by a side, undo, a size cap warning and the result revealed from the original's edges outward.
- `relight-sphere` (Pro): **Relight sphere.** Place the light for a relighting model on a shaded sphere: drag the light over it, past the rim to put it behind the subject, set intensity, softness and an optional color temperature, and preview it on the picture before you render.
- `camera-angle-gizmo` (Pro): **Camera angle gizmo.** Ask for the same scene from another camera angle: drag a camera around a wireframe orbit to rotate and tilt it, pick a distance or a preset, see what it frames, and get the request in plain words for multi-angle models.
- `sketch-pad` (Pro): **Sketch pad.** A drawing surface for sketch to image: brush, eraser, rectangle and ellipse on B, X, R and C, filled or outlined shapes, a palette, line width, undo and redo, pen pressure and touch. Exports black ink on white paper at any size.
- `realtime-canvas` (Pro): **Realtime canvas.** Draw on the left and watch the model answer on the right as you draw: frames sent on change at a capped rate, images swapped without flicker, end to end latency with the last 24 frames, connection states, pause and save frame.
- `text-in-image-edit` (Pro): **Text in image edit.** Edit the text rendered inside an image: every line a detector found gets a box on the image and a field in a list. Type over a line in place, at its size and angle; the result fades in only inside that line while the others stay editable.
- `generation-feed` (Pro): **Generation feed.** Every generation newest first, grouped by prompt: a masonry per prompt under a sticky header, virtualized so thousands of images scroll at full speed, with arrow keys through the masonry and per image actions on hover and focus.
- `generation-lightbox` (Pro): **Generation lightbox.** A full screen viewer for generations: wheel, pinch and double click zoom with drag to pan, arrows between images, side by side compare on one shared view, and the prompt with every parameter next to your actions.
- `variation-actions` (Pro): **Variation actions.** Every next step for a generation in one toolbar: vary subtle or strong, upscale 2× or 4×, remix, use as reference and download, each with a single key shortcut and its own running, done and failed state.
- `quad-grid-select` (Pro): **Quad grid select.** Four results of one prompt in a 2 by 2 grid: press 1 to 4 to pick, U to upscale and V to vary it, or use the U1 to U4 and V1 to V4 buttons, each with its own running, done and failed state.
- `edit-history-strip` (Pro): **Edit history strip.** Every edit to an image as a horizontal strip, from the original to the newest step: undone steps stay visible, forks switch between branches, and any step can be compared with the current version or restored.
- `upscale-compare` (Pro): **Upscale compare.** An original and its upscale on one shared view: drag or key the divider, zoom and pan both sides together, and put the same spot from both images side by side in a split lens with the pixel grid.
- `inpaint-brush` (Pro): **Inpaint brush.** Paint the area an inpainting model should change: brush and eraser with size, feather, invert, undo, redo and clear, pen pressure and touch, and the mask exported as the black and white PNG inpainting APIs expect.
- `imagine-bar` (Pro): **Imagine bar.** The image prompt bar: chips for aspect, style, count, model and seed, typed flags that become chips, a credit cost that follows every change, enhance with undo, reroll and reference images.
- `prompt-builder` (Pro): **Prompt builder.** A structured prompt: subject, style, lighting, lens, composition and mood rows of chips and free text, compiled live into one prompt with copy, weights and edit as text.
- `image-model-picker` (Pro): **Image model picker.** Image models as cards you judge by eye: sample mosaics, speed, a quality meter and cost per image, New and Beta labels, locked models and compare two side by side.
- `resolution-picker` (Pro): **Resolution picker.** Base size and upscale factor with pixel size, megapixels and credit cost on every choice, and plan limits that lock larger choices with an upgrade.
- `batch-stepper` (Pro): **Batch stepper.** Images per run, 1 to 8, with a total credit cost whose digits roll, and a contact sheet of the batch you pick from by clicking a frame, that shows each image arrive.
- `reference-tray` (Pro): **Reference tray.** Style, character and composition references with a weight each: drop files or page images, reorder by drag or keyboard, and see before the drop whether the tray will take it.
- `moodboard` (Pro): **Moodboard.** A saved set of references that travel into prompts together: a masonry you reorder, a name edited in place, a share code, apply to prompt and a chip for the prompt bar.
- `generation-metadata` (Pro): **Generation metadata.** Every parameter behind an image, prompt to timing, with copy one value, copy all, copy as prompt and open in studio.
- `moderation-blur-gate` (Pro): **Moderation blur gate.** A filtered generation blurred past recognition, with the reason, the policy and what you can do: show anyway, confirm age, or request a review.
- `provenance-badge` (Pro): **Provenance badge.** A content credentials badge with a popover of the signed edit history: who signed it, every step since it was made, and a link to inspect.
