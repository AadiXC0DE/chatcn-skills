# Commerce

Agentic shopping: product detail and carousel, cart, variant picker, spend approval, payment method, order tracking, travel and booking cards. Install a free item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn/<name>` and a Pro item with `npx shadcn@latest add @chatcn-pro/<name>`. Full docs for any item: `https://chatcn.dev/docs/components/<name>.md`.

## chatcn Pro

- `product-detail` (Pro): **Product detail.** One product in a message: photos, price, stock and delivery, how it fits what was asked, and one Buy button.
- `product-carousel` (Pro): **Product carousel.** A snapping row of products with receipt style prices, one tab stop and a compare tray.
- `order-receipt` (Pro): **Order receipt.** The receipt after a purchase: lines, totals on leaders, refunds, payment and a return window that drains.
- `order-tracking` (Pro): **Order tracking.** Where a parcel is: the arrival day, a rail filled to the parcel, delivery problems and the scan history.
- `spec-compare` (Pro): **Spec compare.** Products side by side, built around what differs, with the best value ticked and matching specs folded.
- `cart-inline` (Pro): **Cart inline.** A cart in a message: steppers, undo, a rolling subtotal, progress to free delivery and a checkout that names the total.
- `variant-picker` (Pro): **Variant picker.** Size, color and options with stock per combination, a price that follows the variant and a back in stock note.
- `spend-approval` (Pro): **Spend approval.** The agent asks to spend over your limit: what, how far over, why, and a clear approve or decline that expires.
- `price-watch` (Pro): **Price watch.** Watch a price from the thread: its history, how often your alert would have fired, and Buy when it drops.
- `payment-method-picker` (Pro): **Payment method picker.** Saved cards, wallets and bank accounts with masked numbers, expiry warnings and a new card added inline.
- `promo-applied` (Pro): **Promo applied.** A promo code as a ticket stub: the saving, the new total against the old, and codes the agent found.
- `merchant-badge` (Pro): **Merchant badge.** Who you are buying from, small enough for a sentence, with verification, the real site and policy links.
- `refund-request` (Pro): **Refund request.** Return part of an order in the thread: items, reason, where the money goes, and a tracker after.
- `shopping-list` (Pro): **Shopping list.** A list you and the agent keep together: sections, prices and stores, what it added, and Buy remaining.
- `hotel-card` (Pro): **Hotel card.** A stay in a message: photos, dates and room, the total behind the nightly price, and how long refunds last.
- `flight-options` (Pro): **Flight options.** Flights compared on one clock: layovers and problem connections drawn, fares per flight, hold or book.
- `table-booking` (Pro): **Table booking.** A restaurant table picked in the thread: party, seating, a week of days and times that fit the party.
- `ride-quote` (Pro): **Ride quote.** Ride options on one clock from now: the wait, the ride and the arrival, then the driver once booked.
